Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

E.M is a one-person web design business, and the one person is me, Ervin Mosley. This page covers what this website collects, why I need it, and what you can ask me to do with it. It applies to this website (elsharuf107s-ux.github.io/em-webdev) and to emails you send me.

The short version

  • I only get what you choose to send me, through the contact form or by email.
  • I use it to reply to you and, if you hire me, to do the work.
  • I don't sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.
  • This site doesn't use tracking cookies or analytics.
  • You can ask me to see, correct, or delete your information at any time.

What I collect

  • The contact form sends me your name, your business name if you give it, your email address, the package you're interested in, your message and, if you choose to say, how you heard about E.M.
  • If you email me, I get your email address and whatever you write.
  • If you become a client, I keep what I need to build your site and bill you: contact and billing details, the content you send me and our project messages.
  • If I contacted you first, I may have noted your business's publicly listed details (such as business name, phone, email and website) so I could offer my services. You can ask me to stop contacting you and delete them at any time.

Please don't send sensitive information, such as health details, ID numbers or payment card numbers, through the form or by email.

Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a proposal if you ask for one.
  • To deliver and support the work if you hire me, and to keep the business and tax records the law requires.

I won't add you to a newsletter or mailing list without asking first.

Services that handle your information for me

  • Right now the contact form opens your own email app with your message filled in, so it goes straight from you to my Gmail. If I later connect Formspree to the form, Formspree will receive form messages and forward them to my email. It may also process technical details such as your IP address to block spam. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • I receive and store emails, including form messages, in Gmail (Google).
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most hosts, it may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and to keep the site running. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • If you pay me, a payment provider such as PayPal, Stripe or Wise, or your bank, handles the payment. I see your name, email and the amount, but not your full card details.

These services may store data in other countries. I don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't set tracking or advertising cookies and doesn't use analytics. If I add analytics later, I'll update this page first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before anything is set.

If you press "Pause animations", your browser saves that choice (in localStorage, under the name em-motion) so the page remembers it next time. It stays on your device, and nothing about it is sent to me or anyone else.

How long I keep it

  • If an enquiry doesn't turn into a project, I keep it for up to 2 years after we last spoke, then delete it.
  • I keep client records (agreements, invoices and payment records) for as long as tax and business record laws require, which is usually several years.
  • If you ask me not to contact you, I keep a short note of that so I don't contact you again.

Your choices and rights

You can email me at any time to:

  • ask what information I hold about you and get a copy,
  • correct anything that's wrong,
  • ask me to delete it (unless I have to keep it by law, for example invoices), or
  • ask me to stop contacting you.

I'll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local privacy law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Keeping it safe

My accounts use strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and I never ask for passwords by email. No system is completely secure. If I find out your information has been exposed, I'll tell you promptly.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn't aimed at children. I don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If I change how I handle information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
