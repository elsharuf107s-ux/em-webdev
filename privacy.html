Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

E.M is a one-person web design business. This page explains, in plain language, what information this website collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. It covers this website (elsharuf107s-ux.github.io/em-webdev) and emails you send us.

The short version

  • We only get information you choose to send us: through the contact form or by email.
  • We use it to reply to you and, if you hire us, to do the work.
  • We don't sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.
  • This site doesn't use tracking cookies or analytics.
  • You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, business name (optional), email address, the package you're interested in, and your message.
  • Email: whatever you include when you email us, plus your email address.
  • If you become a client: the details needed to build your site and bill you, such as contact and billing details, the content you send us, and our project messages.
  • If we contacted you first: we may have noted your business's publicly listed details (such as business name, phone, email, and website) to offer our services. You can ask us to stop contacting you and delete these details at any time.

Please don't send sensitive information (such as health details, ID numbers, or payment card numbers) through the form or by email.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a proposal if you ask for one.
  • To deliver and support the work if you hire us, and to keep the business and tax records the law requires.

We won't add you to a newsletter or mailing list without asking first.

Services that handle your information for us

  • Formspree receives contact form messages and forwards them to our email. It may also process technical details such as your IP address to block spam. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Gmail (Google) is where we receive and store emails, including form messages.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most hosts, it may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and to keep the site running. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • If you pay us: payment providers such as PayPal, Stripe, Wise, or your bank process the payment. We see your name, email, and the amount, but not your full card details.

These services may store data in other countries. We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't set tracking or advertising cookies and doesn't use analytics. If we add analytics later, we'll update this page first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before anything is set.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become projects: up to 2 years after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client records (agreements, invoices, payment records): as long as tax and business record laws require, which is usually several years.
  • Opt-outs: if you ask us not to contact you, we keep a short note of that so we don't contact you again.

Your choices and rights

You can email us at any time to:

  • ask what information we hold about you and get a copy,
  • correct anything that's wrong,
  • ask us to delete it (unless we have to keep it by law, for example invoices), or
  • ask us to stop contacting you.

We'll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local privacy law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Keeping it safe

We use accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and we never ask for passwords by email. No system is perfectly secure, but if we learn that your information has been exposed, we'll tell you promptly.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn't aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests: elsharuf107s@gmail.com
