Our history

1942

Humble beginnings

Whitmore's BBQ was founded in 1942 as Mt. Pleasant BBQ at 17275 Kinsman Road by Texas native Virgil Whitmore Sr. After the success of the first location, a second opened at 8510 Cedar Road in 1948. The business kept growing through the 1950s, and by 1959 it had four locations, including East 110th Street, St. Clair and Akron.

1963

Growing fast

By 1963, Whitmore closed some of the newer locations to open at 17405 Harvard Ave in the Lee-Harvard area of Cleveland, the city's newest magnet for the Black community. In 1974 he opened a Whitmore's in Hawaii, which ran for four years. In 1978 the Lee-Harvard location closed and a new one opened in the Collinwood neighborhood.

Today

A new generation

For decades, the Whitmore name and the Signature Secret Sauce have been part of Cleveland history. Third-generation siblings Vance and Virgil Whitmore run it now, and it is still one of the city's top barbecue spots. With Vance's culinary school training, Whitmore's is ready for what comes next.
