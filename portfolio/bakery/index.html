Order ahead for pickup

Reserve a couple of loaves, a dozen pastries for the office, or a whole pie for Sunday dinner. We'll bag it and leave it on the pickup shelf with your name on it.

  1. Choose what you want and a pickup time. The earliest is when we open.
  2. Pay online or at the counter.
  3. Come in and take your bag from the pickup shelf.
