Before you sit down

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, every day. We keep at least one chair open for walk-ins, and the screen by the door shows how long the wait is. If you don't want to wait at all, book online.

Can I book a specific barber?

Yes. Pick them in the booking form or tap "Book with" under their name. If you don't mind who cuts it, choose "First available".

How long does a fade take?

Plan on about 45 minutes. That's how long it takes to blend one properly.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Kids twelve and under are $24. Ren does most of our first haircuts, and the booster seat lives at Ren's chair.

What if I need to cancel?

Use the link in your reminder text or give us a call. If you cancel at least two hours ahead, there's no charge.

How do I pay?

Card, tap or cash. You can tip on the card or in the jar by the register.
