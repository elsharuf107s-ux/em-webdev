Questions about getting a website

How much does a website cost?

Starter (1 page) is $299, Business (up to 5 pages) is $699 and Growth (6 to 10 pages, with booking or ordering integration, SEO setup and analytics) is $1,199. These are one-time build fees. The optional care plan is $29 a month.

How long does it take?

Most sites go live 7 to 10 business days after I have your text, photos and logo. The sooner you send them, the sooner the site is live.

Do you only work with businesses in Ohio?

No. I'm in Ohio, but I work remotely with small businesses anywhere in the US. We can do everything by email, or on a video call if you'd rather talk.

How do payments work?

The preview is free. You pay a 50% deposit after you approve the preview and sign. If you cancel in writing within 2 business days of signing, I refund the whole deposit. The other 50% is due at launch. The care plan is billed monthly, and you can cancel it any time.

What's included in the $29/month care plan?

Hosting checks, updates and up to 30 minutes of small text or photo edits each month. You can cancel any time.

What if I need changes the care plan doesn't cover?

Extra work is $50 an hour. I'll give you an estimate first, and I don't start until you say yes.

Can my site take online bookings?

Yes. Any package can include a simple "Book now" button that links to the booking page you already use. Setting up booking or ordering inside your site is part of the Growth package.

Do I need to pay for hosting?

For most simple sites, no. I can put your site on free static hosting. If you want your own domain name (like yourbusiness.com), you pay the registrar for it directly, often $10 to $20 a year.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once the build is paid for, the site and its content are yours, and I'll hand over all the files if you ever want to move.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, photos of your work or your premises, and the basics: services, prices and hours. If you're short on good photos or text, I'll help.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes, and I'll show you how. If you'd rather not, the optional $29/mo care plan lets you email me your changes instead.

What is the founding client discount?

E.M is new, so my first 3 clients get 30% off the build fee: Starter $209, Business $489, Growth $839. I'll ask for an honest testimonial, but it's optional and you keep the discount either way. I don't give discounts or anything else in exchange for Google reviews.

Are the businesses in the Work section your clients?

Not yet. Parcer00's Barbershop, Martin 3rd Generation Construction and Jenns Home and Office Cleaning are real businesses, but they haven't hired me. I built those previews on my own to show what I'd make for them. When I have paid client work, it will be labeled as client work.

Will my site show up on Google?

I set up every site so Google can read it, with page titles, descriptions, a layout that works on phones and a link to your Google Business Profile. I can't promise where you'll rank, and nobody else honestly can either, but the basics will be done right.
