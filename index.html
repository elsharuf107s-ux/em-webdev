FAQ

Questions about getting a small business website

How much does a website cost?

Starter (1 page) is $299, Business (up to 5 pages) is $699 and Growth (6 to 10 pages, with booking or ordering integration, SEO setup and analytics) is $1,199. These are one-time build fees. The optional care plan is $29 a month.

How long does it take?

Most sites go live 7 to 10 business days after I receive your content: your text, photos and logo. The sooner I have those, the sooner you're live.

Do you only work with businesses in Ohio?

No. I'm based in Ohio and work remotely with small businesses across the United States. Everything can be done by email, or by video call if you'd like to talk it through.

How do payments work?

A 50% deposit to start and 50% at launch. If you cancel in writing within 2 business days of signing, before work starts, you get the full deposit back. The care plan is billed monthly and you can cancel any time.

What's included in the $29/month care plan?

Hosting checks, updates and up to 30 minutes of small text or photo edits each month. You can cancel any time.

What if I need changes the care plan doesn't cover?

Extra work is $50 an hour. I'll tell you the estimate before I start, and nothing begins until you say yes.

Can my site take online bookings?

Yes. Any package can include a simple "Book now" button that links to the booking page you already use. Setting up booking or ordering inside your site is part of the Growth package.

Do I need to pay for hosting?

For most simple sites, no. I can host your site on free, reliable static hosting. If you want your own domain name (like yourbusiness.com), you pay the registrar directly, usually a small yearly fee.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once the build is paid for, the site and its content are yours. I'll hand over all the files if you ever want to move.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo (if you have one), photos of your work or premises, and basic details like services, prices and hours. Don't have good photos or text? I'll help you with that.

Can I update the site myself?

You can, and I'll show you how. Or join the optional $29/mo care plan and send me your changes by email.

What is the founding client discount?

E.M is new, so my first 3 clients get 30% off the build fee: Starter $209, Business $489, Growth $839. I'll ask for an honest testimonial, but it's optional and the discount stays either way. I never offer discounts or anything else in exchange for Google reviews.

Are the bakery and plumber sites real clients?

No. Golden Crumb Bakery and FlowRight Plumbing are concept projects for fictional businesses, made to show my design and build quality. E.M is new, and real client work will be labeled as such.

Will my site show up on Google?

Every site is set up so Google can read it: page titles, descriptions, a mobile-friendly layout and a link to your Google Business Profile. I can't promise rankings, and nobody honestly can, but I'll make sure the basics are done right.
