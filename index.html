FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take?

Most Starter and Business sites go live in about one to three weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly we can gather your text and photos. Growth sites can take a bit longer.

Do I need to pay for hosting?

For most simple sites, no. We can host on free, reliable static hosting. If you want your own domain name (like yourbusiness.com), you pay the registrar directly, usually a small yearly fee.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once the build is paid for, the site and its content are yours. We'll hand over all the files if you ever want to move.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo (if you have one), photos of your work or premises, and basic details like services, prices and hours. Don't have good photos or text? We'll help you with that.

Can I update the site myself?

You can, and we'll show you how. Or join the optional $29/mo care plan and send us your changes by email.

What is the founding client discount?

E.M is new. Our first clients get a 30% launch discount. We'll ask for honest feedback and whether we can show the finished site in our portfolio, but that's optional. Ask us for details when you get in touch.

How do payments work?

Typically half up front to start and half at launch. The care plan is billed monthly and you can cancel any time.
